What do those words mean?

Here are a few answers to get started.

What is filament?

The printer’s material. Each spool has a color.

What does AMS mean?

Bambu Lab’s system that automatically switches between spools.

What is a 3MF file?

The file containing your model and its colors for Bambu Studio.

Does BlendParser print my model?

It prepares the model. Bambu Studio handles print preparation.
