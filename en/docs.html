From your first file
to a prepared model.

A step-by-step guide to BlendParser.

Reviewed for alpha-0.0.5. Screenshots were captured from the official 0.0.5 executable. The example uses a Bambu Lab A1, a 0.4 mm nozzle and a height of 75 mm.

Your first conversion

BlendParser converts a Blender model with materials or textures into a 3MF painted by filament. Then open that file in Bambu Studio to prepare the print.

  1. Install Blender 3.6 or later and download BlendParser 0.0.5 for Windows or Linux.
  2. Drop a .blend file or use File > Open .blend….
  3. Choose the printer and nozzle in the top bar.
  4. Adjust the final dimensions and check the plate and purge tower.
  5. Compare Original and Result, adjust the palette and paint if needed.
  6. Click Export .3mf and open the file in Bambu Studio.

You do not need to build the project to use the installers. Blender must be installed to read the model.

Installation, language and updates

Windows and Linux

On Windows choose .exe or .msi; the app uses WebView2. Linux offers AppImage, Debian (.deb) and RPM. Enable execution permission before opening an AppImage. Keep Blender 3.6 or later installed.

The 0.0.5 installers do not carry Windows code signatures and may trigger SmartScreen. App updates are checked with their own signature; these are two different signatures.

Choose the language

Open Edit > Preferences… and choose Español or English. The change applies immediately and is remembered when you reopen the app. On first launch, the first supported system language is used; otherwise it defaults to English. Numbers follow a regional format compatible with the selected language.

Check for new versions

The app checks for updates on launch. You can also click the version or use Help > Check for updates. Export your work before installing: the app restarts. On Linux, built-in updates apply to AppImage; update Debian and RPM by downloading another package.

Prepare your Blender file

Save a normal .blend. Files such as .blend1, .blend2 are backups: open them in Blender and save them as .blend before importing.

Extraction reads the scene’s visible meshes and evaluates their modifiers. Color can come from an image with UV coordinates, vertex color attributes or a flat material color.

  • Check that the meshes you want to convert are visible.
  • If you use images, make sure Blender can find them and that the model has UVs.
  • Check the scene units, then confirm the height in millimeters inside BlendParser.

The result is not a Blender render: lighting, reflections and complex node combinations may look different. The extractor looks for material color sources; it does not reproduce all shading. For an image without UVs, it may use an average color.

Printer, size and measurement

Choose a printer and nozzle

The catalog includes 87 single-nozzle printers from Bambu Studio profiles, including brands such as Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality and Anycubic. Use the top-bar search and choose an available nozzle. The profile supplies the build volume, excluded areas and process layer height.

In Edit > My printers… you can create a printer with its own name, volume and nozzles, optionally linked to a catalog profile. Your selection and custom printers are saved for future sessions. Dual-extruder printers are outside the catalog.

Change dimensions

Edit Width (X), Depth (Y) or Height (Z) in millimeters. With Proportional enabled, the other dimensions follow. Unlock it to stretch each axis separately. Percentage scale is relative to the original model and restores its proportions.

Use Original size to reset dimensions or Undo resize to restore the previous size. The Size (S) tool lets you drag axis handles in the viewport; the black handle scales the entire piece. Labels show dimensions as you drag; use the Size panel fields to enter an exact value. Press L to toggle the proportion lock.

Measure on the piece

Activate Measure (M). Before the first point, the model height is shown. Click two points: the ruler follows the cursor and displays the distance in millimeters and its X, Y and Z components. Hold Shift when placing the second point to measure along one axis. A third click starts a new measurement; Esc or Clear measurement removes it.

Flatten the base

Enter the cut in millimeters and click Flatten or press Enter. The range is 0 to 50 mm; 0 disables the cut. Start with a small amount and check the plate contact.

Plate and purge tower

Open Plate and tower to see the model footprint, margin, excluded areas and tower reservation. The check also accounts for available height.

  • Reserve space for the purge tower: sets space aside when checking and fitting the size.
  • Tower depth: leave it empty for an estimate or enter a manual value.
  • Gap and margin: set the distance between the piece and tower and the plate edge margin.

Use Fit to plate if there are size warnings. Fitting respects margins, exclusions and the reservation. Press P to show or hide the plate in the viewport.

The reservation is an estimate of space. Bambu Studio generates the actual tower; check its position and the sliced result before printing.

Colors and AMS slots

Detect a palette

In Detect again with you can choose 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 or 12 colors. The palette is calculated from the model. Adjust each target color to the filaments you have: the app does not automatically read the spools in your printer.

Edit a slot

The circle opens the color picker. The card selects that slot for painting and activates the brush. It also shows its hex color, triangle count and closest matching slot.

Merge colors

Merge closest down to reduces the palette to the specified number, preserving hand-painted work and allowing undo. You can also use Merge into… to choose the destination for a specific slot.

Before detecting again: recalculating the palette or clicking Suggest colors again discards manual painting. If you have already painted, the app asks for confirmation. To reduce colors while keeping your painting, use color merging.

Choosing more colors in the app does not add physical slots to your equipment. Review your filament configuration in Bambu Studio.

Viewer and 3D brush

Original shows the source color; Result shows the filament colors. Switch between them to judge how much reducing the palette changes the appearance.

Estimate waste for your model

BlendParser estimates how much filament the model may waste during printing. Use this figure as a guide to assess the material needed before printing and compare different sizes, colors or detail settings. The calculation accounts for nozzle, layer height and direction of color changes.

The selected nozzle limits boundary detail and enables warnings about color spots too small to print. The initial layer height comes from the profile; you can adjust it in the waste dialog.

Read the breakdown

Expand estimated waste to see color-change purge, initial purge and tower, plus filament length and volume. Purge reused in infill or supports is shown separately and does not add to waste.

Adjust estimation assumptions

Open Edit > Waste settings… or the button in the breakdown. Match the estimation parameters to your print: layer height, material, density, filament diameter, purge multiplier, initial purge, priming and reused percentage.

Read the purge table from the row slot to the column slot, in mm³ before the multiplier. Switching from dark to light may need more purge than the reverse. Edited values are marked and exported to the 3MF. Use Back to the worked-out values to reset them; Save settings keeps preferences for future models.

The estimate does not replace slicing. Bambu Studio's final color order, supports, tower and purge may produce different figures. A printer without a purge chute deposits that purge in the tower without counting it twice.

Repair and simplify the mesh

Repair mesh merges duplicate vertices and removes degenerate triangles and repeated faces. The summary shows what was fixed or whether the mesh was already clean.

Remove loose parts smaller than removes small components based on their size. Leave it at 0 to keep them, and check that increasing it does not remove a detail you want.

Simplify controls the percentage of geometry to keep: 100% keeps the mesh, and lowering it reduces the triangle count. Check the silhouette and color boundaries; too much simplification can remove details you want to print.

Automatic repair does not guarantee that every model becomes printable. Also review the result and warnings in Bambu Studio.

Export the result

Multicolor 3MF

Click Export .3mf or Ctrl + S, choose where to save it and open it in Bambu Studio. The file includes per-filament color and the corresponding printer, nozzle and purge settings. Check filaments, profile, orientation, tower and slicing before printing.

Export by color

In 0.0.5, By color (.zip) is hidden because separate-part export is still incomplete. The assembly kit and separate color STLs are not available from this release's interface. The available output is multicolor 3MF.

Troubleshoot common issues

Blender cannot be found

Install Blender or use Find blender.exe when the app offers it. The app also supports the BLENDER_PATHenvironment variable. Reload the model after setting the path.

The file has no visible meshes

Open the file in Blender, check that visible mesh objects exist and save your changes. The extractor does not convert cameras or lights into parts.

Colors do not match

Check materials, images and UVs in Blender. Compare Original with Result: a palette difference can be corrected by adjusting slots; a texture that was not read needs checking in the source file.

The model is too large

Select the correct printer and use Fit to plate. If you only change the printer profile, check the size warning again.

Purge differs from Bambu Studio

This is an estimate for comparing settings. The slicer calculates the print with its own color sequence and purge parameters.

I cannot update

Check your connection and click the version to check again. You can also download an installer from the official releases. Export your work before installing or restarting.
